sQUIZit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

sQUIZit (winf-os.github.io/sQUIZit) turns a photo of notes or textbook pages into a ready-to-take exam. This page explains, plainly, what data the app touches and where it goes.

The short version

  • sQUIZit has no server-side accounts and no backend database. There is nothing for sQUIZit itself to leak, sell, or lose, because it doesn't hold onto your data centrally in the first place.
  • Your Gemini API key and your exam library live only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device.
  • Google Drive backup, if you turn it on, can only see and manage a single backup file it creates itself — never anything else in your Drive.

Your Gemini API key

sQUIZit is "bring your own key" — you create your own free Google Gemini API key and paste it into the app. It's saved only in your browser's localStorage and sent, per request, to a Supabase Edge Function that forwards it to Google's Gemini API on your behalf. sQUIZit's server does not store, log, or retain your key at any point — it only passes through on each individual request.

Your exam content

Photos or text you provide to generate an exam are sent (via the same proxy) to Google's Gemini API to produce questions, and are not separately stored by sQUIZit afterward. The exams you create — questions, your answers, scores, attempt history — are saved only in your browser's local storage, on your own device, unless you explicitly export or back them up yourself (see below).

Backup & Restore

Profile → Backup & Restore offers two ways to keep a copy of your exam library, both opt-in:

  • On this device: downloads a plain JSON file to your phone or computer. This file never leaves your device unless you move it yourself.
  • Google Drive: if you connect your Google account, sQUIZit can save/restore that same backup as a single file in your own Drive. This uses Google's drive.file OAuth scope, which restricts the app to only the files it creates itself — sQUIZit cannot see, read, or modify any other file in your Google Drive, and cannot access your Drive at all unless you explicitly tap Connect.

What sQUIZit does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no analytics cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties, because sQUIZit never holds a copy of it to begin with.

Third-party services involved

  • Google Gemini API — processes the source material you submit to generate exam questions and grade essay answers, under Google's own privacy terms.
  • Supabase — hosts the lightweight Edge Functions that relay requests to Gemini; it does not persist your API key or your exam content.
  • Google Drive API — only if you opt in to Drive backup, scoped as described above.
