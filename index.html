      About this app

      QuizForge turns a photo of your notes or textbook pages into a ready-to-take exam using Google Gemini. Question generation runs through a Supabase Edge Function so the AI key stays off your device. Prefer not to use AI? Manual Build and Auto-Extract on the Create screen work fully offline of Gemini.

      Data

      Your exam library (completed exams and drafts) is saved in this browser's storage and stays there across visits — see the Library tab. Clearing your browser data will remove it.
